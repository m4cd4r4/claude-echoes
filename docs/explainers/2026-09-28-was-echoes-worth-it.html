As a tool you use: not yet. As a lab: yes. It mostly told us what not to build.

1. Nobody is using it

When a tool goes dark for two days and nothing you do changes, it wasn't part of how you work.

2. Search is only half good

Right = the answer is in the top 5 results. Simple: 0.633 on 34 questions (run f7b, 20 Sep). Spread: 0.215 (run f2, 19 Sep). The biggest fix was to the test, not the search: the old test picked its answers wrongly. Full story: Where Echoes Stands.

3. Can your past picks choose for you? No.

Your history doesn't predict your next choice. So "auto-pick for me" and "you chose X last time" hints are both dead.

4. Learning from being told off

New rules: rerun only the items that failed, not the whole batch. Never start a command you hand over with &.
